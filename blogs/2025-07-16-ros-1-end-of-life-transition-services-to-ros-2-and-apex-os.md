For teams still relying on ROS 1, the shift to ROS 2 is no longer just a recommendation—it’s a necessity. ROS 2 introduces key improvements that make it the clear successor to ROS 1. These include middleware abstraction, communication, deterministic execution, and modular system design—essential capabilities for building modern, scalable, and secure robotic systems.
