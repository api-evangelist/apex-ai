Physical AI brings machine learning into systems that sense, decide, and act in the world. Autonomous vehicles, mobile robots, agricultural machines, and other intelligent devices increasingly use learned models to interpret their surroundings and determine what to do next. An end-to-end model can simplify the path from sensor input to an action.
